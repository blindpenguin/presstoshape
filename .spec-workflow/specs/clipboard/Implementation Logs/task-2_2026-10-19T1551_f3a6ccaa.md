# Implementation Log: Task 2

**Summary:** Added document helpers for copying and placing shapes: with_fresh_ids (recursive through Composite parts), add_on_top (active layer even if hidden, or entered Composite with modes), insert_copies (each container's copies directly above its topmost original), and delete_selected (shared by Delete and Cut).
